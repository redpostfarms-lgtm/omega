## SHOP SAFETY RULES (OSHA-COMPLIANT)

### General Shop Rules
1. Always wear appropriate PPE
2. No loose clothing, jewelry, or long hair near machinery
3. Keep work area clean and organized
4. Know location of emergency exits and equipment
5. Report all accidents and near-misses immediately
6. Never work alone with dangerous equipment
7. No horseplay or distractions
8. Use tools only for intended purpose
9. Inspect tools and equipment before use
10. Follow manufacturer instructions
11. Keep aisles and walkways clear
12. Proper storage of materials and tools
13. Adequate lighting for all work areas
14. Proper ventilation for fumes and dust
15. First aid kit accessible and stocked

### Auto Shop Specific Rules
1. Vehicle properly supported (jack stands, never just jack)
2. Engine off and keys removed when working
3. Battery disconnected for electrical work (LOTO)
4. Cooling system cooled before opening
5. No smoking near fuel or flammable materials
6. Spill kits available for oil/fuel spills
7. Proper disposal of used oil and fluids (EPA compliant)
8. Ventilation for exhaust fumes
9. Eye protection when grinding or cutting
10. Hearing protection for loud operations
11. LOTO for hybrid/electric vehicle work
12. High voltage safety for EV batteries
13. Fire extinguisher rated for Class B
14. Proper lifting techniques
15. Tool inspection before use
