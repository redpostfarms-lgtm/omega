## Logo Design

### Central Element: Red Glossy Omega Symbol (Ω)
- **Color**: Vibrant deep red (#C41E3A or similar)
- **Finish**: Highly reflective, glossy/metallic appearance
- **Style**: Bold, thick, sans-serif
- **3D Depth**: Volumetric with specular highlights and shadows
- **Lighting**: Light source from top-front creates highlights on upper surfaces

### Surrounding Element: Golden Laurel Wreath
- **Color**: Rich lustrous gold (#FFD700 or similar)
- **Finish**: Polished metal appearance with strong reflections
- **Form**: Symmetrical circular arrangement around Omega
- **Detail**: Individual overlapping leaves with pointed tips
- **3D Depth**: Highlights on outer edges, shadows in overlapping areas

### Background: Dark/Black Textured
- **Color**: Very dark charcoal/black
- **Texture**: Subtle vertical brushed/scratched texture
- **Purpose**: Ensures Omega and wreath stand out prominently

---

## Usage

### 1. BIOS Boot Logo
- **File**: `boot_logo/omega_logo.bmp`
- **Size**: 1024x768 pixels
- **Format**: BMP (UEFI compatible)
- **Colors**: Red Omega, Gold Wreath, Dark Background

### 2. Desktop Shortcut Icon
- **File**: `omega_icon.ico`
- **Sizes**: 16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 256x256 pixels
- **Format**: ICO (Windows icon format)
- **Purpose**: Desktop shortcut icon for Omega

**Color Scheme:**

**Composition:**
- Omega symbol centered
- Golden wreath encircles Omega
- Dark background provides contrast
