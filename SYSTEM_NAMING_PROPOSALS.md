# System Naming Proposals - Defense & Attack System

**Current Name:** Quantum Armor  
**Purpose:** Rename the defense and attack system

---

## Naming Criteria

The system should reflect:
- Stealth/invisibility (VPN cloaking, zero fingerprint)
- Quantum effects (decoherence, entanglement)
- Defense/attack capabilities
- Military/tactical nature
- Self-defense and counterstrike
- Professional/technical feel

---
