SAP S/4HANA Modules Comparison (2026 Overview):

**Key Concept:** SAP S/4HANA uses Lines of Business (LoBs) instead of traditional ECC modules

**Core LoBs (Standard):**
- Finance - Financial accounting, controlling, treasury, Universal Journal
- Sales - Order-to-cash processes
- Procurement - Operational procurement, supplier management
- Manufacturing - Production planning, MRP, shop floor control
- Supply Chain - Logistics, inventory, transportation, EWM, TM

**Extended LoBs:**
- Asset Management - Equipment and facility maintenance
- R&D/Engineering - Product lifecycle management, PLM
- Human Resources - Talent and workforce management
- Service - After-sales service

**Industry-Specific LoBs:**
- Retail (fashion)
- Oil & Gas
- Utilities
- Public Sector
- Automotive

**Extended Solutions (Cloud Add-ons):**
- SAP Ariba (procurement)
- SuccessFactors (HR)
- Concur (expenses)

**Key Takeaway:** S/4HANA's strength is real-time integration across LoBs via Universal Journal and embedded analytics, unlike ECC's siloed modules.
