### Overview
- Multi-year initiative (2018–ongoing)
- Digital core for 320k+ employees across 190+ countries
- SAP S/4HANA-based standardization
- Phased, agile approach with pilots

### Five Phases

#### Phase 1: Preparation & Planning (2018–2019)
- Key activities: Legacy assessment, business case, roadmap
- Focus: Identified pain points, data silos
- Milestones: ONE.ERP platform definition, S/4HANA selection
- Duration: ~1 year

#### Phase 2: Design & Blueprinting (2019–2020)
- Key activities: Process design, global templates, integration planning
- Focus: Harmonized processes, AI integration
- Milestones: Global templates, Siemens Xcelerator integration
- Duration: ~1 year

#### Phase 3: Realization & Build (2020–2022)
- Key activities: Configuration, migration, custom development
- Focus: S/4HANA with Central Finance elements, SLT integration
- Milestones: Pilot go-lives (Siemens Gamesa), real-time testing
- Duration: ~2 years

#### Phase 4: Deployment & Go-Live (2022–2024)
- Key activities: Phased rollouts, cutover, hypercare
- Focus: Expansion to more business units, AI-driven features
- Milestones: Unified reporting, MindSphere integration
- Duration: ~2 years

#### Phase 5: Run & Optimize (2024–Ongoing)
- Key activities: Monitoring, AI enhancements, expansions
- Focus: Industry 4.0, predictive analytics, sustainability
- Milestones: AI maturity by 2027, TCO reduction
- Duration: Perpetual

### Conclusion
ONE.ERP's phased approach enables progressive standardization with minimal disruption, strong AI focus, and integration beyond finance.
