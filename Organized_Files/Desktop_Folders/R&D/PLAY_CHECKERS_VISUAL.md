## How to Play

### Launch Visual Board
```bash
python elara_visual_checkers.py
```

### Controls
- **Left Click**: Select piece or make move
- **Click Piece**: Highlights piece, shows legal moves (green)
- **Click Destination**: Moves piece, switches turn
- **Close Window**: Exit game

### Game Features
- ✅ **800x800 Window** - Dark walnut background, gold trim
- ✅ **Red-Black Squares** - Breathing effect, smooth colors
- ✅ **Click to Move** - Select piece, click destination
- ✅ **Legal Move Highlights** - Green squares show valid moves
- ✅ **Smooth Animations** - Pieces slide, crowns float
- ✅ **Beginner AI** - Plays like 10-year-old, makes mistakes
- ✅ **Status Display** - Shows current turn, difficulty level

## Beginner Mode Features

- 30% chance of making mistakes
- Sometimes misses jumps
- Leaves pieces open
- Random move selection (not optimal)
- Smack talk: "Almost had it!", "Close!", "Good try!"

---

## Game Flow

1. **Red moves first** (bottom rows)
2. **Click red piece** → Highlights yellow, shows green legal moves
3. **Click green square** → Piece moves, turn switches to black
4. **AI moves automatically** → Black piece moves (beginner style)
5. **Repeat** → Until game ends
