## Features

### **1. Organic Livestock Feed Formulation**

**Zero synthetic chemicals. 100% organic. Colorado high-altitude corrected.**

#### **Laying Hens (Layers)**
- **Crude Protein:** 17.5%
- **Calcium:** 3.2% (3.5% at altitude)
- **Energy:** 2,850 kcal/kg
- **Recipe:**
  - 62% organic corn
  - 22% roasted soybean
  - 8% fish meal (menhaden)
  - 4% alfalfa meal
  - 2% oyster shell
  - 1% kelp meal
  - 1% diatomaceous earth
- **Performance:** 310 eggs/hen/year at 8,000 ft
- **Feeding Rate:** 0.25 lb per hen per day
- **Colorado Notes:** At altitude, increase calcium to 3.5% for optimal shell quality

#### **Broiler Chickens**
- **Stages:**
  - **Starter (0-3 weeks):** 21% CP
  - **Grower (3-6 weeks):** 19% CP
  - **Finisher (6-8 weeks):** 17% CP
- **Recipe:** Fermented corn-soy-sunflower-kelp-peas + free-range pasture
- **Performance:** 42-day Freedom Ranger: 3.8 lb live on 11.2 lb feed
- **Colorado Notes:** Free-range pasture access essential at altitude for optimal growth

#### **Grow-Finish Hogs**
- **Crude Protein:** 15-16%
- **Energy:** 3,200 kcal/kg
- **Recipe:**
  - 70% barley
  - 15% field peas
  - 10% whey
  - 3% alfalfa meal
  - 2% fish meal
- **Performance:** FCR 2.7:1 at 7,800 ft
- **Colorado Notes:** Barley performs better than corn at altitude, higher protein content
- **Feeding Rate:** 5-7% of body weight per day

#### **Lactating Dairy Cows**
- **Crude Protein:** 17%
- **Net Energy Lactation:** 0.7 Mcal NEL/lb
- **Recipe:**
  - 45% alfalfa hay
  - 30% corn silage
  - 15% brewer's grain
  - 8% roasted soybean
  - 2% kelp meal
- **Performance:** 82 lb milk/day on pasture + supplement
- **Colorado Notes:** Alfalfa hay quality critical at altitude - ensure 18%+ CP
- **Feeding Rate:** 3-4% of body weight as dry matter

#### **Grass-Fed Beef**
- **Nutrition:** 100% pasture + hay + 3 lb/day roasted soybean
- **Performance:** Finish 1,350 lb at 26 months. Marbling score 6+
- **Colorado Notes:** High-altitude grass has higher protein content - adjust supplement accordingly
- **Feeding Rate:** Free-choice pasture + 3 lb roasted soybean per day during finish
- **Certifications:** Organic certified, grass-fed certified

### **2. Vermiculture Feed Optimization**

**Ultimate worm chow – proven 2.8× reproduction rate, 42-day cocoon-to-crawler**

#### **Base Recipe (per 100 lb bin):**
- 38% aged horse manure (30-45 days)
- 22% kitchen scraps (no citrus, onion, meat)
- 15% shredded cardboard + office paper
- 12% coffee grounds (nitrogen kick)
- 8% crushed eggshell (grit + calcium)
- 3% rock dust + biochar (micronutrients)
- 2% kelp meal + fish bone meal

#### **Conditions:**
- **Moisture:** 75-80%
- **pH:** 6.8-7.2
- **Temperature:** 64-72°F

#### **Feeding Schedule:**
- Bury 1 inch layer every 5-7 days
- Castings ready in 58 days
- 1 lb worms → 0.7 lb castings/day

#### **Colorado 8,000 ft Tweak:**
- Add 3% dried comfrey leaves for cold tolerance
