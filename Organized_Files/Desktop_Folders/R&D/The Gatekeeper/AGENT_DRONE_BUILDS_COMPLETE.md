## Agent Drone Designs

### 1. Alpha - SPEED RACER 🏎️

**Design Philosophy:** Maximum speed, high responsiveness, lightweight

**Components:**
- **Frame**: 450mm carbon fiber (lightweight)
- **Motor**: SunnySky X2212 2300KV (high RPM)
- **ESC**: T-Motor Flame 30A BLHeli_32 (fast response)
- **Battery**: 4S 1500mAh 40C (high voltage, low weight)
- **Propeller**: 10x4.5 Carbon Fiber (small, efficient)

**Performance:**
- **Total Thrust**: 2000g
- **Total Weight**: 794g
- **TWR**: 2.52:1
- **Max Speed**: 37.6 m/s (135 km/h)
- **Flight Time**: 1.8 minutes
- **Payload**: 200g
- **Agility Score**: 0.90
- **Cost**: $432.00

**Design Rationale:**
- High KV motor (2300KV) for maximum RPM
- 4S battery for high voltage (14.8V)
- Small 10" props for speed
- Lightweight frame for agility
- Optimized for tag and dodgeball games
