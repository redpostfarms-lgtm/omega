## Colorado HR Laws 2026 (Loaded)

### **Wage Act:**
- Minimum wage 2026: $14.42
- Overtime: 1.5x after 40 hours
- Final paycheck: Same day for termination, next payday for resignation

### **HFWA (Healthy Families & Workplaces Act):**
- 80 hours PSL per year
- Accrual: 1 hour per 30 hours worked
- Maximum carryover: 80 hours
- No payout on termination

### **Equal Pay for Equal Work Act:**
- Salary range posting required on every job ad
- Prohibited salary history inquiries

### **FAMLI (Family and Medical Leave Insurance):**
- 0.9% payroll tax (split 50/50 employee/employer)
- Employee contribution: 0.45%
- Employer contribution: 0.45%

### **CROWN Act:**
- Protected characteristics: hair texture, hairstyle

### **Marijuana Accommodation:**
- Off-duty use protected
- Accommodation required

### **Whistleblower Protection:**
- Protection for reporting violations

### **COBRA:**
- Mini-COBRA: 2-19 employees
- Standard COBRA: 20+ employees
