# SHOGI RULES

## Board
Board: 9×9 grid (81 squares)

## Pieces
- King (Gyoku/Osho) - 1 each
- Gold General (Kin) - 2 each
- Silver General (Gin) - 2 each
- Knight (Keima) - 2 each
- Lance (Kyosha) - 2 each
- Bishop (Kaku) - 1 each
- Rook (Hisha) - 1 each
- Pawn (Fu) - 9 each

## Initial Setup
Row 1 (Back rank, Black): Lance, Knight, Silver, Gold, King, Gold, Silver, Knight, Lance
Row 2 (Black): Bishop on left, Rook on right
Row 3 (Black): 9 Pawns
Rows 4-6: Empty
Row 7 (White): 9 Pawns
Row 8 (White): Rook on left, Bishop on right
Row 9 (Front rank, White): Lance, Knight, Silver, Gold, King, Gold, Silver, Knight, Lance

## Movement Rules
- King: One square in any direction
- Gold: Forward, backward, left, right, forward diagonals (6 directions)
- Silver: Forward, forward diagonals, backward diagonals (5 directions)
- Knight: Two squares forward, then one left/right (L-shape, forward only)
- Lance: Forward only, any distance
- Bishop: Diagonals, any distance
- Rook: Horizontals and verticals, any distance
- Pawn: One square forward only

## Promotion
- Pieces promote when entering opponent's territory (last 3 rows)
- Promoted pieces have different movement patterns
- King and Gold cannot promote

## Capture and Drop
- Captured pieces join your hand
- Can drop pieces from hand on empty squares (no checkmate on drop)
- Pawns cannot be dropped in checkmate

## Win Condition
Checkmate the opponent's King

## Special Rules
- Cannot drop a piece to create immediate checkmate
- Pawns cannot be dropped on files that already contain unpromoted pawns
- Two kings cannot occupy same position
