Accenture's Role in EDF SAP S/4HANA Central Finance Project:

**Overview:**
- Accenture served as primary implementation partner for EDF's major SAP S/4HANA Central Finance deployment
- Described as one of largest and most ambitious Central Finance projects to date
- Collaboration focused on modernizing EDF's core finance solution
- Goals: Better integration, real-time capabilities, enhanced agility in financial processes

**Key Contributions:**

1. **Project Leadership and Execution:**
   - Collaborated closely with EDF to design, build, deploy new SAP S/4HANA platform
   - Central Finance as centerpiece
   - Migrated data from five SAP source systems
   - Handled initial load of 1.5 billion documents

2. **Core Implementations:**
   - Deployed SAP S/4HANA Central Finance
   - Enabled seamless integration between shared services centers and corporate finance entities
   - Adopted SAP Business Process Consolidation (BPC) for streamlined group reporting
   - Provided full real-time financial capabilities
   - Allowed employees to access data from initial postings in source systems through to statutory consolidation

3. **Management Framework:**
   - Implemented new rules and controls
   - Aligned with evolving business objectives
   - Ensured compliance and flexible steering

4. **Decommissioning Legacy Systems:**
   - Helped EDF address obsolescence in older systems
   - Maintained flexibility and coverage

**Overall Outcomes Enabled by Accenture:**
- Optimized financial closing processes
- Improved audit trails, accountability, data analysis
- Delivered greater consistency, data sharing, proactive problem-solving for finance teams

**Partnership Value:**
- Highlights Accenture's expertise in large-scale, complex Central Finance transformations
- Particularly relevant in utilities sector where real-time data and regulatory compliance are critical
