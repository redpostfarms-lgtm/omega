Real-world SAP S/4HANA Central Finance Implementation Case Studies (2021-2025):

**Case Study 1 - EDF (Électricité de France):**
- Largest CFIN deployment
- Partner: Accenture
- Scale: Migrated 5 SAP source systems + 1.5 billion documents
- Achievements: CFIN + Business Process Consolidation, better integration, compliance framework
- Outcome: Enhanced agility in financial closing, real-time visibility

**Case Study 2 - Fonterra:**
- World's Largest Dairy Exporter
- Partner: insightsoftware
- Challenges: Multiple disparate systems post-acquisitions
- Results: Consistent performance tracking, robust global reporting, competitive advantage

**Case Study 3 - Perrigo:**
- Consumer Healthcare Giant
- Partner: PwC/TruQua
- Landscape: 21 separate ERP systems (SAP + non-SAP)
- Approach: Two releases, foundation first (CFIN + Group Reporting + BPC/SAC)
- Go-Live: Early 2021
- Benefits: Reduced reconciliation, real-time insights, foundation for broader rollout

**Case Study 4 - Sanlam:**
- South African Financial Services
- Partner: Syniti/insightsoftware
- Goal: Intelligent operations via streamlined data integration
- Outcome: CFIN as core for real-time reporting and compliance

**Case Study 5 - Swiss Post:**
- Multi-ERP Consolidation
- Approach: Reduced from 11 ERP + 9 BW systems to single S/4HANA instance
- Benefits: Real-time transparency, 30% TCO reduction, centralized processes

**Common Lessons Learned:**
- Data Harmonization Critical (MDG essential)
- Phased Approach Wins (reporting → Central Payments → full processes)
- Initial Load Challenges (dedicated SLT servers needed)
- Real-Time Replication (SLT + AIF key)
- Change Management (Fiori training needed)
- ROI Timeline: 12-24 months typical, 50-70% faster closing

**Key Insight:** Central Finance is low-risk, high-value path to S/4HANA, especially for complex multi-system landscapes.
