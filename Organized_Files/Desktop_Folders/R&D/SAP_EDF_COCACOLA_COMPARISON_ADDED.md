## Content Added

**Title:** SAP S/4HANA Central Finance Comparison: EDF vs Coca-Cola Bottlers

**Content Includes:**

### Project Overview
- **EDF (with Accenture):**
  - Global energy utility (€143B revenue, 160k+ employees)
  - 5 SAP source systems
  - 1.5 billion documents initial load
  - Multi-year timeline
  - Primary goal: Modernize finance, real-time integration, closing agility
  - Key components: S/4HANA CFIN + Business Process Consolidation

- **Coca-Cola Bottlers (with Infosys, 2024-2025):**
  - Multinational beverage bottler (300+ legal entities, 9,200+ users)
  - 20-year legacy system migration
  - Phased rollout completed ~2025
  - Primary goal: Digital transformation, unified finance, supply chain readiness
  - Key components: S/4HANA CFIN + Central Payments + new GL template

### Challenges
- **EDF:** Massive data volume (1.5B documents), 5 legacy SAP systems, shared services vs corporate finance integration, energy regulations + multi-GAAP compliance, replication strain on high-volume utility
- **Coca-Cola Bottlers:** High master data changes across 300+ entities, mix of SAP and non-SAP ERPs, global payroll/tax + supply chain integration, international tax/payroll/export compliance, parallel run of old ECC + new CFIN

### Outcomes
- **EDF:** Full real-time from posting to consolidation, enhanced closing agility, better audit trails, proactive problem-solving, flexibility, reduced reconciliation effort, foundation for broader S/4HANA
- **Coca-Cola Bottlers:** Consistent global reporting, streamlined interfaces, automated payments to 75 countries, faster month-end close, agility for supply chain phased rollout, simplified master data, lower TCO, enabled future supply chain optimizations

### Key Differences
- **Industry Focus:** EDF (utilities) emphasized real-time visibility and regulatory steering; Coca-Cola Bottlers (consumer goods) prioritized payment automation and global payroll/tax
- **Scale Type:** EDF = document volume; Coca-Cola Bottlers = entity count + international complexity
- **Approach:** EDF focused on consolidation; Coca-Cola Bottlers added Central Payments heavily
- **Partner Role:** Accenture handled massive scale; Infosys emphasized operational automation

### Conclusion
Both showcase CFIN as a low-risk path to S/4HANA—EDF for raw volume, Coca-Cola Bottlers for global operations.
