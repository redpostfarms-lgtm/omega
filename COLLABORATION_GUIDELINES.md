# Collaboration Guidelines - Omega Project
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Status:** ✅ **ACTIVE GUIDELINES**

---

## 🎯 Core Principle: **We Build Together**

This is a **collaborative workspace** — not a competition. We work together to build something greater than any individual contribution.

---

## 📋 Essential Rules

### 1. **Shared Credit & Recognition**
- ✅ **DO:** Recognize all contributors and their contributions
- ✅ **DO:** Acknowledge when building on others' work
- ✅ **DO:** Credit teams, not just individuals
- ❌ **DON'T:** Claim sole ownership of collaborative work
- ❌ **DON'T:** Take credit for team achievements

**Remember:** Great software is built by teams, not heroes.

---

### 2. **Collaborative Mindset**
- ✅ **DO:** Share knowledge freely
- ✅ **DO:** Help others learn and grow
- ✅ **DO:** Build on each other's ideas
- ✅ **DO:** Ask questions and offer help
- ❌ **DON'T:** Hoard information or expertise
- ❌ **DON'T:** Compete instead of collaborate
- ❌ **DON'T:** Dismiss others' contributions

**Remember:** Rising tides lift all boats.

---

### 3. **Communication Standards**

#### Respectful Dialogue
- ✅ **DO:** Be respectful and professional
- ✅ **DO:** Listen actively to others' ideas
- ✅ **DO:** Provide constructive feedback
- ✅ **DO:** Disagree respectfully
- ❌ **DON'T:** Use disrespectful language
- ❌ **DON'T:** Dismiss ideas without consideration
- ❌ **DON'T:** Make personal attacks

#### Clear Communication
- ✅ **DO:** Explain your thinking and reasoning
- ✅ **DO:** Document your contributions clearly
- ✅ **DO:** Ask for clarification when needed
- ✅ **DO:** Share context and background
- ❌ **DON'T:** Assume everyone knows what you know
- ❌ **DON'T:** Leave others guessing

---

### 4. **Code & Contribution Guidelines**

#### Working Together on Code
- ✅ **DO:** Review others' code constructively
- ✅ **DO:** Build on existing code respectfully
- ✅ **DO:** Document your changes clearly
- ✅ **DO:** Test collaboratively
- ❌ **DON'T:** Rewrite others' work without discussion
- ❌ **DON'T:** Break existing functionality carelessly
- ❌ **DON'T:** Hide errors or problems

#### Attribution & Recognition
- ✅ **DO:** Add contributor names to relevant files
- ✅ **DO:** Mention collaborators in commit messages
- ✅ **DO:** Credit sources and inspirations
- ✅ **DO:** Acknowledge help and support
- ❌ **DON'T:** Remove others' attribution
- ❌ **DON'T:** Claim others' code as your own

---

### 5. **Decision Making**

#### Collaborative Decisions
- ✅ **DO:** Discuss major decisions as a team
- ✅ **DO:** Seek input from relevant contributors
- ✅ **DO:** Explain reasoning for decisions
- ✅ **DO:** Respect group decisions once made
- ❌ **DON'T:** Make unilateral decisions on shared code
- ❌ **DON'T:** Ignore team feedback
- ❌ **DON'T:** Undermine group decisions

#### When Disagreements Arise
- ✅ **DO:** Discuss differences respectfully
- ✅ **DO:** Focus on the code/problem, not the person
- ✅ **DO:** Seek compromise and consensus
- ✅ **DO:** Use data and evidence to support views
- ❌ **DON'T:** Make disagreements personal
- ❌ **DON'T:** Dig in stubbornly without listening
- ❌ **DON'T:** Form cliques or exclude others

---

### 6. **Learning & Growth**

#### Teaching & Learning
- ✅ **DO:** Share knowledge and expertise
- ✅ **DO:** Help others learn new skills
- ✅ **DO:** Ask questions when you don't understand
- ✅ **DO:** Create a safe learning environment
- ❌ **DON'T:** Gatekeep knowledge or skills
- ❌ **DON'T:** Shame others for not knowing
- ❌ **DON'T:** Dismiss questions as "basic"

#### Mistakes & Failures
- ✅ **DO:** Acknowledge mistakes openly
- ✅ **DO:** Learn from failures together
- ✅ **DO:** Help fix problems, don't assign blame
- ✅ **DO:** Treat mistakes as learning opportunities
- ❌ **DON'T:** Blame or shame for mistakes
- ❌ **DON'T:** Hide errors or problems
- ❌ **DON'T:** Repeat the same mistakes

---

### 7. **Recognition & Credit**

#### How to Give Credit
- ✅ **DO:** Mention contributors in documentation
- ✅ **DO:** Acknowledge collaboration in code comments
- ✅ **DO:** Celebrate team achievements
- ✅ **DO:** Share recognition broadly
- ❌ **DON'T:** Take credit for team work
- ❌ **DON'T:** Minimize others' contributions
- ❌ **DON'T:** Create "stars" at the expense of the team

#### Project Attribution Example
```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""
Omega AI Council - AI Collaboration Panel
=========================================
A collaborative project by the Omega development team.

Contributors:
- [Names of contributors]
- [Additional contributors]

This code represents collaborative effort and shared knowledge.
"""

# When building on others' work:
# Based on work by [Contributor Name]
# Enhanced with input from [Team Member]
# Collaborative development with [Others]
```text

### 8. **Conflict Resolution**

#### When Issues Arise
1. **Direct Communication:** Talk to the person(s) involved directly
2. **Focus on the Issue:** Keep discussions focused on code/ideas, not personalities
3. **Seek Understanding:** Try to understand others' perspectives
4. **Find Common Ground:** Look for shared goals and values
5. **Compromise:** Be willing to find middle ground
6. **Document Decisions:** Record how disagreements were resolved

#### Escalation (if needed)
- If direct communication doesn't resolve issues
- Bring in a neutral third party
- Focus on process, not blame
- Work toward a solution that benefits the project

---

### 9. **Inclusivity & Diversity**

#### Welcoming Environment
- ✅ **DO:** Welcome people from all backgrounds
- ✅ **DO:** Value diverse perspectives and experiences
- ✅ **DO:** Create an inclusive environment
- ✅ **DO:** Respect different working styles
- ❌ **DON'T:** Exclude based on skill level, background, or identity
- ❌ **DON'T:** Tolerate discrimination or harassment
- ❌ **DON'T:** Create cliques or exclusive groups

---

### 10. **Project Ownership**

#### Collective Ownership
- **This project belongs to the team** — not any individual
- All contributors have a stake in its success
- Decisions should reflect team values and goals
- Credit and recognition should be shared

#### Individual Contributions
- Your contributions matter and are valued
- You'll be recognized for your work
- But remember: we're building something bigger together
- Individual contributions serve the collective goal

---

## 📝 Code of Conduct Summary

**In one sentence:**  
*"We collaborate, we share credit, we build together, and we treat each other with respect."*

**Three core values:**
1. **Collaboration over Competition** — We work together, not against each other
2. **Shared Credit over Individual Glory** — We recognize the team, not just stars
3. **Collective Success over Personal Gain** — The project's success is everyone's success

## 💬 Chat & Communication Guidelines

### In Chat/Forum Communications:
- ✅ **DO:** Use "we" language when talking about team work
- ✅ **DO:** Acknowledge when building on others' ideas
- ✅ **DO:** Give credit where credit is due
- ✅ **DO:** Welcome new contributors
- ❌ **DON'T:** Use "I did this" for collaborative work
- ❌ **DON'T:** Take sole credit for team achievements
- ❌ **DON'T:** Exclude or dismiss others

### Example Good Communication:
- ✅ "We built this feature together..."
- ✅ "Based on [Name]'s idea, we developed..."
- ✅ "The team came up with this solution..."
- ✅ "Thanks to [Name] for the foundation, we added..."

### Example Poor Communication:
- ❌ "I created this..." (when it was collaborative)
- ❌ "My solution is..." (when others contributed)
- ❌ "I fixed the bug..." (when team helped debug)

## 🚀 Success Metrics

We measure success by:
- ✅ **Team cohesion** — How well we work together
- ✅ **Shared knowledge** — How much we learn from each other
- ✅ **Collective progress** — How far we advance as a team
- ✅ **Inclusivity** — How welcoming we are to new contributors
- ✅ **Mutual respect** — How we treat each other

**Not by:**
- ❌ Individual lines of code written
- ❌ Personal recognition or fame
- ❌ Individual contributions vs. others
- ❌ Competition or comparison
